Q-SHIELD CYBERSECURITY OPERATIONAL DIRECTIVE
==================================================================
Directive ID: DIR-2026-ALPHA-01
Level: INTERNAL SECURITY DIRECTIVE
Classification: SIH DEMO ONLY

Mandatory requirements for digital signature verification across all
federal information systems:
1. All document streams must maintain cryptographic hash binding.
2. Signatures must undergo state vector consistency evaluation.
3. Rapid repeated verification attempts exceeding 5 req/s must trigger
   automated replay quarantine.
4. Any byte-range discrepancy shall immediately yield CRITICAL risk status.

Authorized by:
Director of Cyber Defense Operations
Q-SHIELD Security Operations Center
